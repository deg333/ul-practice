Power formulas (click to insert)

DC circuits

P = V × I   dc_vi(v, i)
P = I² × R   dc_ir(i, r)
P = V² ÷ R   dc_vr(v, r)

AC single phase (pf = cos θ)

P = V × I × cos θ   ac1_vi(v, i, pf)
P = I² × R × cos θ   ac1_ir(i, r, pf)
P = V² ÷ R × cos θ   ac1_vr(v, r, pf)

AC 3-phase

P = √3 × VL × IL × cos θ   ac3_l(vl, il, pf)
P = 3 × VP × IP × cos θ   ac3_p(vp, ip, pf)
P = 3 × I² × R × cos θ   ac3_ir(i, r, pf)
P = 3 × (V² ÷ R) × cos θ   ac3_vr(v, r, pf)

Results are in watts. Power factor is cos θ: give it directly (0.8) or as cos(36.87) (degrees). Formulas are as printed in the source table; for a purely resistive load use pf = 1.

How to use the pad

Given – one per line: V_L = 480 V, I_ph = 17.3, pf = 0.8. Names may have subscripts with _ (V_L, I_{ph}); a unit after the number is kept for the answer. Later lines can use earlier ones.

Find – what you want: P = ? W. The answer is boxed at the bottom of the Solution when an equation defines it.

Equations – top to bottom, each can use any variable above it: P = sqrt(3) * V_L * I_L * pf. Typeset steps appear in the Solution: the formula, then your numbers substituted, then the result.

Operators: + − * / ^ %, brackets, sqrt(), sin/cos in degrees, Greek names (theta, eta), and all the calculator’s helpers (up(), flc3(), i3(), wire() …). Click a variable chip to insert its name. Notes after # are ignored.
